= Why We Use Antora
:toc:
:icons: font
:author: kazutoiris

Multiple documentation generation frameworks exist in the ecosystem. For example, https://docs.readthedocs.com/platform/stable/intro/doctools.html[Read the Docs] supports MkDocs, Sphinx, Docusaurus, Markdoc, mdBook, VitePress, and Antora. Our evaluation criteria prioritize 3 core requirements:

. Content-focused authoring
+
We require plain text composition with minimal abstraction layers. Solutions like VitePress and Docusaurus were excluded due to their web development-oriented architecture and inherent complexity in content abstraction.
. Flexible documentation structure
+
While Sphinx, MkDocs, and mdBook provide robust documentation frameworks, their hierarchical chapter/section enforcement proves overly restrictive for specification documentation requiring non-linear content organization.
. Expressive semantic markup
+
Although Markdoc offers customization capabilities, we selected AsciiDoc as our primary format due to its native support for advanced documentation features.

This combination of semantic richness and structural flexibility makes Antora particularly suited for our needs.
