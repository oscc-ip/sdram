= Why we use Antora
:toc:
:icons: font
:author: kazutoiris

Multiple documentation generation frameworks exist in the ecosystem. For example, https://docs.readthedocs.com/platform/stable/intro/doctools.html[Read the Docs] supports _MkDocs_, _Sphinx_, _Docusaurus_, _Markdoc_, _mdBook_, _VitePress_, and _Antora_. Our evaluation criteria prioritize 3 core requirements:

. Content-focused authoring
+
We require plain text composition with minimal abstraction layers. Solutions like _VitePress_ and _Docusaurus_ were excluded due to their web development-oriented architecture and inherent complexity in content abstraction.
. Flexible documentation structure
+
While _Sphinx_, _MkDocs_, and _mdBook_ provide robust documentation frameworks, their hierarchical chapter/section enforcement proves overly restrictive for specification documentation requiring non-linear content organization.
. Expressive semantic markup
+
Although _Markdoc_ offers customization capabilities, we selected _AsciiDoc_ as our primary format due to its native support for advanced documentation features.

This combination of semantic richness and structural flexibility makes Antora particularly suited for our needs.
